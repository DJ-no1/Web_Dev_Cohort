# Fitness Platform

## Overview
A full-stack platform connecting trainers and clients with coaching, content delivery, tracking, and payments.

---

## Core Features

### Identity and Roles
- Users can be Trainer, Client, or Admin  
- Trainers create and manage programs  
- Clients subscribe and track their progress  

### Messaging
- Direct messaging between trainer and client  
- Enables communication and support  

### Products and Payments
- Trainers can create:
  - Subscriptions  
  - Cohorts  
  - Digital products  
- Clients can:
  - Subscribe or purchase  
  - Make one-time or recurring payments  

### Events and Sessions
- Trainers schedule:
  - One-on-one sessions  
  - Group or cohort sessions  
- Clients join using meeting links  

### Content Delivery
- Trainers upload assets such as PDFs, videos, and links  
- Content is unlocked based on product access  

### Reviews
- Clients can rate and review products  

### Progress Tracking
- Daily check-ins:
  - Energy, stress, steps, sleep  
  - Habit tracking  
- Progress logs:
  - Weight, body fat, measurements  
  - Progress photos  

### Trainer Insights
- Trainers can add notes for each client  
- Notes can be private or shared with the client  

---

## Workflow

1. Trainer signs up and creates products  
2. Client signs up and subscribes to a product  
3. Client gains access to content and events  
4. Client logs daily progress and updates  
5. Trainer monitors progress and provides feedback  
6. Payments are handled through subscriptions or one-time purchases  

---

## Goal

To build a scalable coaching ecosystem combining content, communication, commerce, and health tracking.
